How it works

  1. Write the stick

    Any USB stick of 4 GB or more. How

  2. Boot it

    Hold Option as the Mac powers on and pick OmaBoot?.

  3. Test

    A quick pass first: about two minutes, no input. Startup, graphics acceleration, display, battery, audio chip, Wi-Fi, temperatures and fans, keyboard backlight.

    Then, if you have a few minutes more, hands-on tests: keyboard, trackpad, speakers, headphones, microphone, camera, Bluetooth, the ports (a drawn map shows where to plug in), external display, lid, charging and sleep. Skip any you like.

  4. Report

    Send the results to {{DIO}}. They're reviewed there and count toward the verdict for your Mac's configuration. No network? Save the report to the stick and send it later.

Leaves your disks untouched

Before anything else, every internal disk is set read-only. Nothing is installed and no firmware or startup setting changes. Pull the stick and your Mac is as it was.

Real Omarchy, not a lookalike

Built from Omarchy's own ISO, with the same kernels and packages as an official release. Each release shows exactly what differs. Check it

Your exact Mac

One model often comes in several configurations, with a different graphics chip, Wi-Fi card or screen. OmaBoot? Live identifies the exact one, and your results count for it. It gets online by cable, Wi-Fi or a phone on USB tethering, or tests offline.
