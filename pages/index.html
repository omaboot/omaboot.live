Your exact Mac, not just the model

One model identifier can cover several different machines. MacBook Pro (15-inch, Early 2011) [MacBookPro8,2] came with either a Radeon HD 6490M or a 6750M, and the same identifier's Late 2011 models brought a 6770M: three graphics chips that Linux drives differently.

OmaBoot? Live reads what your Mac really has: its model identifier and board ID, its graphics, Wi-Fi and other chips by their IDs, and finds the one configuration that matches, out of 206 across 121 Mac models. When two could fit, you pick yours from a short list.

Your results then count for that exact configuration on DoesItOmarchy.com, so the next person with the same Mac knows what to expect.

Your disks stay untouched

Before anything else, every internal disk is set read-only. Nothing is installed and no firmware or startup setting changes. Pull the stick and your Mac is as it was.
